    Register of amendments — English translation¶

    Translation. This is an English translation of AMENDMENTS.md, made on 2026-10-27 after the freeze. The Italian file is the frozen, authoritative text: its pre-freeze part is hashed in study/FREEZE.md and checked by scripts/verify_freeze.sh. If the two disagree, the Italian wins and this file gets fixed. New post-freeze entries are written in English in AMENDMENTS.md and copied here.

    The protocol is frozen at the OSF deposit (Phase 1). From that moment every change must be recorded here with date, reason and consequence, and reported in the final report.

    Before the freeze¶

    2026-09-01 — v0.1 -> v0.2, outcomes of Phase 0¶

    All the following changes predate the deposit and come from Phase 0, which did not touch any real event data.

    # Change Reason
    1 Primary statistic of H1: from Watson U^2 to the directional harmonic cos(n*delta) One degree of freedom instead of two, against the exact alternative that astrology predicts. More powerful, and it stays independent of the orb
    2 The Sun leaves the primary test Structural confounding with seasonality: a surrogate that preserves the season also preserves the Sun
    3 Surrogates: from +/-[7,365] days to whole years +/- 20 days up to +/-6 years Preserves season and clock time, decorrelates everything else
    4 Surrogates: GLOBAL shift, not per event A per-event shift destroys the autocorrelation of the sky and inflates false positives
    5 Surrogates: from 1000 to 400 Constraint of the +/-6-year window. p-value resolution 1/401, enough for alpha 0.01
    6 Estimator: regression on anomalies filtered at 90 days Cuts the background noise by a factor of 3 without losing signal
    7 Unfiltered variant DISQUALIFIED False positives 0.040 against 0.010 nominal, with realistic counts
    8 MEI: from 0.01 (eyeballed estimate) to 0.0306 (explicit derivation) The derivation gives C_Gauquelin = 0.0611. Corrected before knowing the MDE; external inputs only
    9 Moon computed topocentrically Measured parallax 0.62 degrees, not negligible with a 3-degree orb
    10 Phase 2a added: recalibration on the real counts Power depends on the structure of the counts. Looking at the autocorrelation of Y without pairing it with the sky is not peeking

    2026-09-02 — v0.2 -> v0.3, after the author's objection on the estimator¶

    The author asked for the choice of estimator to be re-examined. The review found a diagnostic error in v0.2 and changed three things.

    # Change Reason
    11 Surrogate radius from +/-6 to +/-40 years; surrogates from 400 to 1000 THIS WAS THE CAUSE. At +/-6 years nearby shifts produce almost identical skies: the null does not sample the low-frequency variability and the test becomes anticonservative. False positives at alpha 0.01: 0.043 at +/-6 years, 0.017 at +/-15, 0.003 at +/-40
    12 High-pass filter REMOVED from the primary With the corrected null it is no longer needed. v0.2 had promoted it to primary, treating the symptom instead of the cause
    13 Unfiltered variant reinstated The v0.2 disqualification was an artefact of the too-narrow radius, not a property of the estimator
    14 Reporting per pair class mandatory The aggregate MDE of 0.0088 is dominated by the Moon. Per class: Moon 0.0094, Mercury 0.0249, slow pairs 0.0276. On the slow pairs the margin over the MEI is 10%, not threefold
    15 Market series promoted to a long-history primary arm The only way to gain power on the slow pairs: more independent slow configurations. GDELT does not go back before 2015, the markets do

    Note on the v0.2 error. The power comparison that made the filter look like the winner injected a UNIFORM effect on all 14 pairs. In that case the filter "wins" because it implicitly reweights the statistic towards the lunar pairs, which are the ones with the lowest noise. Injecting the effect within a single class and estimating it with that class, the filter is neutral (MDE ratios 0.99, 1.00, 1.03): the estimator is a normalised regression, so filtering removes signal and noise in proportion. The apparent advantage was an artefact of the uniformity assumption.

    2026-10-27 — v0.3 -> v0.4, delegation to the autonomous program¶

    Context. The PO delegated the continuation of the study to an autonomous program (a cloud routine every 5 hours, program/CHARTER.md) and asked it to take the decisions still open by itself. The following decisions are taken today, before any sky–event pairing, with declared rules. None depends on a result about the sky: the only information about the data used is marginal counts (T5), which the protocol declares allowed (V9). After this entry the protocol is frozen as v0.4 (study/protocol-v0.4.json, SHA-256 in study/FREEZE.md).

    # Change Reason
    16 GDELT thresholds: NumSources ≥ 4, NumMentions ≥ 25; \|Goldstein\| ≥ 5, ActionGeo_Type ≥ 2 and lat/lon present stay T5: in the GDELT 2.0 export files sources and mentions count only the first 15 minutes; the v0.3 thresholds gave 3.8 events/day, not 25. Rule declared before choosing: keep Goldstein and geocoding fixed, choose the (S, M) pair of the measured grid closest to 25/day. Outcome 27.0/day (3 full days); the neighbour S≥5,M≥25 gives 18.3 over 3 days and 19.9 over 10, confirming the grid. Declared semantics: "event already on at least 4 sources and 25 mentions in the first quarter hour"
    17 GDELT event date: SQLDATE, counting a row only if the file that contains it is dated SQLDATE or the day after. Dedup on GLOBALEVENTID GDELT includes rows about old events recalled by new articles. A 48-hour window keeps the day's events and discards historical recalls. DATEADDED is never the time of the event
    18 Markets arm (SPEC V § 8.3): option A only. S&P 500 index (^SPX, Stooq), daily closes from the first available date ≥ 1928-01-01 to the day before the freeze. Daily outcome y_d = \|ln(C_d / C_{d−1})\|, trading days only (closed days are absent, not zero) No paid intraday provider: the autonomous program makes no purchases. Option A gives what the arm was promoted for (AMENDMENTS #15): a century of slow configurations. The estimator is a normalised regression and holds for any positive series
    19 Surrogates of the markets arm: same principle as V6 (radius ≈ 3.5 × the window), so whole years j ∈ ±[1, 340] with shift round(365.2425·j) + u, u ∈ ±20, 1000 surrogates. Calibration mandatory on 400 pseudo-real windows before the pairing (Phase 2a); if it fails, the arm leaves the primary analysis and this is declared ±40 years is 3.5 times 11.5 years; the markets window is ~98 years. Over 340 years the 365-day year would drift by 82 days and break the season, so the year is the mean Gregorian one. Ephemeris: mundane profile (1200–2399)
    20 H3 (angular channel) is tested on USGS only, the only dataset with an exact time. H4 is computed on the aspects channel (H1) only: GDELT against USGS Without intraday data the human arm has no timestamps. Declared as a limitation, not worked around
    21 H5 (semantic channel) is not run by the autonomous program It needs external embedding models and semantic cleaning of the descriptions, outside the numeric perimeter (V3). Holm stays at m = 5: hypotheses not run count as p = 1. Conservative
    22 Split. Unit: calendar month (not day, not event), over all months from 1928-01 to 2026-09. Stratified by year: even years 7/3/2 months, odd years 7/2/3 (exploration / validation / historical prospective), ≈ 58/21/21. numpy PCG64 generator, seed 20260902. Every dataset uses the same split. data/split/months.csv and the SHA-256 of the three lists in study/FREEZE.md Nearby days have almost identical skies and correlated counts (autocorrelation 30–70 days): a split by day would leak exploration into validation. The month limits the leakage to the edges, and stratifying by year avoids a different "planetary era" between the sets (protocol § 2.5). October 2026 is the month of the freeze and goes entirely to the prospective set
    23 Prospective set: the historical 20% plus all days from 2026-10-01 onwards (the month of the freeze and the following ones). It opens not before 2027-04-27 (6 months) Protocol § 2.5
    24 Use of the sets. No statistic involving the sky reads y from a set other than the one analysed. The filtered variants (robustness) are computed within the set, monthly block by block Filtering the whole series would pass information from validation to exploration
    25 Stopping rule. The program ends with the Phase 6 report, whatever the outcome. Confirmation is a single opening of the validation set with the primary hypotheses exactly as here. Exploratory analyses stay on the exploration set only, are labelled as such with FDR (BH), and an outcome of theirs becomes a result only if it replicates on the prospective set The PO asked to keep going "until something significant is found". Put that way, it is the forking-paths search that the protocol exists to prevent. This is the sound form of the same request: the program keeps going until the answer, and a null is an answer
    26 Internal freeze. Protocol v0.4 is frozen with a commit, tag protocol-v0.4 and SHA-256 hashes pushed to GitHub on 2026-10-27. The OSF deposit stays with the PO; until it exists, every result carries the label "internal pre-registration, not deposited" The autonomous program does not create accounts and does not publish. A hash in a private repo dates the protocol for those with access, but does not count as a deposit with a third party
    27 One p-value per hypothesis, for Holm (m = 5). Every sub-test uses the global time-shift null with 1000 surrogates, one-sided p as in the protocol. H1 = Bonferroni over 8 tests: harmonics {2, 3, 4, 6} × arms {GDELT, markets}, aggregate statistic over the 14 pairs: p_H1 = min(1, 8·min p). Reporting per class (Moon, Mercury, slow) and the USGS arm are mandatory but descriptive, outside Holm. H2 = monotone trend over the orb bands (statistic monotone_trend_z, negative tail), arms {GDELT, markets}: p_H2 = min(1, 2·min p). H3 = USGS diurnal resultant, harmonics m ∈ {1, 2} × fast bodies {topocentric Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars}: p_H3 = min(1, 8·min p); circumpolar cases excluded and counted. H4 is computed only if H1 or H3 is rejected by Holm, otherwise p_H4 = 1: it is the shift test of the difference δ̂_GDELT − δ̂_USGS at the harmonic that gave the minimum p in H1, positive tail. H5: p_H5 = 1 Protocol v0.3 fixed five hypotheses and Holm, but did not say how each one's sub-tests become a single p. Leaving it open until the data would be the most dangerous choice of the study. Bonferroni within the hypothesis is conservative and simple. With 1000 surrogates the minimum p is 1/1001, and 8/1001 = 0.008 stays below the strictest Holm threshold (0.01): the design can still reject

    After this entry the protocol is frozen. Every later change goes under "After the freeze" with date, reason and consequence, and cannot touch primary hypotheses, estimator, surrogates, thresholds, split or stopping rule.

    After the freeze (v0.4)¶

    None of these entries touches primary hypotheses, estimator, surrogates, thresholds, split or stopping rule.

    # Date Change Reason and consequence
    28 2026-10-27 Source of the markets arm: same series (S&P 500, daily closes), taken from Yahoo Finance (^GSPC, API v8/finance/chart) instead of Stooq (^SPX) Stooq now answers with a JavaScript anti-bot check, which the program does not bypass. The estimated series is the same: the index's official closes. First date 1928-01-03, last 2026-10-26, 24,821 days. study/protocol-v0.4.json stays as it is: its source field counts as history, and this entry corrects it
    29 2026-10-29 GDELT primary filter: one calibration on the real counts before the pairing. Phase 2a (S2.3) found the GDELT primary filter (none) not calibrated under the S2.2 generator: 0.070 at α 0.05, 0.020 at 0.01, KS p 0.017 (results/phase2a/summary.json). The frozen text does not say what happens when the primary filter of a primary arm fails. Before any statistic that pairs GDELT y with the sky, the Phase 2a calibration (armCalibration in engine/src/mundane/phase2a.ts) is repeated once with the real GDELT counts in place of the synthetic ones. Procedure. y: the real daily counts (#16, #17), exploration-set days only (#22, #24); the other days are absent, as in analyze --set exploration. Sky: 400 pseudo-real windows on the frozen calibration grid (CALIB_GRID_START 1880-01-01 → CALIB_GRID_END 2150-12-31); day k of the arm reads the sky of window start + k; harmonic 2, primary filter (none), the frozen Tier 1 shifts (1000) for each p, as in Phase 2a. No window may start within 40 years of the real GDELT start (2015-02-19), so no window comes near the real sky. Random stream: the engine's Rng, root seed 20261029. Criterion: summarizeCalibration unchanged (rate at 0.05 ≤ 0.05 + 3·SE + 0.005, rate at 0.01 ≤ 0.01 + 3·SE + 0.005, KS p > 0.02). Output results/phase2a/calibration-gdelt-real.json. Run once: no other seed, grid, exclusion or harmonic. Rule, declared before running. PASS → GDELT stays in H1 and H2 with the frozen primary filter, as frozen. FAIL → GDELT leaves the confirmatory analysis, as a non-calibrated markets arm would (#19): its H1 and H2 sub-tests count as p = 1, the Bonferroni factors stay 8 and 2, H4 (GDELT against USGS) counts as p = 1 in Holm (as #21 does for H5), and GDELT is analysed and reported as descriptive, with every filter. Either way the report gives both calibrations, synthetic and real Reason. The S2.2 generator is deliberately wider than the real series (synthetic variance +37%, slow AR φ 0.995), so its failure may come from the generator and not from the data. Real counts paired with the skies of other epochs keep H0 true by construction and test the frozen procedure on the data it will be applied to. Exploration days only: the validation and prospective sets stay unread (#24). Limits. The 400 windows share one y and overlap in sky, so they are less independent than the synthetic draws; the tolerance is not adjusted for this. Consequence. Estimator, primary filter, surrogates, thresholds, split and stopping rule do not change. This is the only post-freeze entry that can change which arms enter H1 and H2, and only in the FAIL branch, by the rule already declared for markets in #19, which can only lower false positives. Decided by the PO on 2026-10-29 after seeing the synthetic calibration only: no statistic pairing GDELT y with the sky had been computed
