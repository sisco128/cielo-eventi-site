    What I could not check¶

    • Whether an ID keeps its SQLDATE across export files (finding 6), and so how rare the warm-up route really is. That needs the files.
    • When GDELT publishes the 23:45 file (finding 5). R2.2's 00:15 margin is the RFC's estimate and no source is cited.
    • The size of any revision or of any difference. The record shows none in month 1, and measuring one would read y.
    • The deployed workflow run's logs and the Actions quota. They are not visible from this session.
