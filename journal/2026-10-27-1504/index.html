    Decisions made (before running what depends on them)¶

    • Log-rate transformation: log(n + 0.5) for counts, log(y) with y > 0 for markets. Reason: the simplest one that handles the zeros; it only serves to estimate generator parameters and does not touch any confirmatory estimate (§ 4.5).
    • φ reported in three ways (ρ1, exponential fit of the ACF, and with the drift removed by a moving average), because ρ1 is attenuated by count/observation noise and the ρ2/ρ1 estimator is unstable (markets: 1.047, outside [0,1]; it stays in the JSON as is). Which one to use is up to S2.2; the planned conservative criterion: the most persistent structure (more dependence = wider null).
    • ACF lags by row index: for markets they are trading days, not calendar days (written in the JSON).

    Problems¶

    • GDELT: the drift is not linear in the log (185 → 147 → 72 → 39 → 34 per year); the linear ramp leaves structure in the residual. The weekly amplitude (0.33) is above the assumed 0.20 and not sinusoidal (ACF at 7 > ACF at 1). S2.2 must reproduce the shape of the drift, not just its amplitude.
    • Markets: long-memory volatility (ACF still 0.09 at 400 days); a single AR(1) represents it poorly. To keep in mind in the calibration on 400 windows.
