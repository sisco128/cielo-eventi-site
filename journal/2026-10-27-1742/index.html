    • Wrote scripts/phase2a_generator.py (numpy/scipy only, reads only the three daily series, no engine module, no grid: V9). It estimates the generator parameters per arm and writes them to results/phase2a/generator.json.
    • Model of the log-rate (counts) or of the log-volatility (markets), per row: linear ramp with random sign + annual sinusoid with random phase + day-of-week profile (7 levels; 5 for markets) + slow AR(1) + fast AR(1). Counts: conditional NB rescaled to the arm's mean. Markets: y = exp(l)·|ε|, ε ~ N(0,1), rescaled to the mean, Monday–Friday only.

    • Implemented the same generator in TS: engine/src/mundane/synthetic.ts (drawArm, armLogStructure, syntheticDays), with an optional tilt β·(c − mean c) on the log scale as in counts.ts. Tests engine/tests/unit/mundane-synthetic.test.ts (6): the moments of the TS draws match those of the Python twin recorded in the JSON, determinism, trading days without Saturday/Sunday, direction of the tilt.

    Decisions made (before running what depends on them)¶

    • Drift and long memory as a slow AR(1), not as the real curve. Copying the real yearly means would have put real y into the generator (forbidden by the plan). The slow component has a random sign/realisation. Reason: § 4.2, the simplest that reproduces the measured ACF (fit rmse ≤ 0.009 on all arms).
    • Weekly as a per-day profile, with a random rotation if the dates are not given. Reason: S2.1 showed that for GDELT it is not sinusoidal.
    • NB k = min(k on the log scale, k on the raw scale). Calibrating on the log residual alone gave USGS k = 6.0 with synthetic raw variance 1.78 against 3.78 real; on the raw scale GDELT is already above the real variance even with Poisson (k = ∞). The smaller k is kept: more dispersion, wider null (§ 4.2, conservative). Implementation detail of the generator: it does not touch any confirmatory estimate.
    • Markets: Gaussian |ε| noise with no free parameters; the 313 days with y = 0 are not reproduced (the generator produces no zeros).
    • The parameter JSON lives in results/phase2a/ and the engine tests read it from there; S2.3 will use drawArm with those parameters, not the frozen COUNTS_MODEL (which stays in the protocol as the value assumed in Phase 0).

    Problems¶

    • USGS: the real lag-1 ACF (0.159, next-day aftershocks) is not reproduced (0.019): an AR(1) on the log-rate does not make aftershock swarms. The daily variance is reproduced.
    • Markets: synthetic variance 37% below the real one (Gaussian tails lighter than real returns) and ACF1 0.23 against 0.32. To keep in mind in the S2.3 calibration: if the arm turns out calibrated with this generator, it is not guaranteed to be so with heavier tails.
    • GDELT: synthetic raw variance +37% over the real one and lag-7 ACF 0.73 against 0.87: the synthetic drift is wider than the real one, which is conservative.
