    Decisions made (before running what depends on them)¶

    • Data days = the arm's whole calendar window, not the validation days. Reason: the plan says "Phase 0 … with the measured parameters", and Phase 0 used the whole window. The confirmatory sets are smaller (about 2–3 months in 12), so the real MDE will be larger: this goes in the report as a limitation.
    • Gate read at the primary filter (none), not at the best filter as Phase 0's verdict did. Reason: the primary filter is fixed by v0.4 (HP_WINDOW_PRIMARY = null). This is the more conservative reading. by_class is computed at the primary filter even when calibration disqualifies that filter, so that the number exists.
    • Power also at the H1 per-test level α/8. Under amendment #27, p_H1 = 8·min p, so at Holm's first step a single test needs p ≤ 0.00125, which with 1000 surrogates means p = 1/1001. Phase 0's α = 0.01 overstates the confirmatory power. This is descriptive only, and the gate stays on Phase 0's definition. It does not change any random stream: the α = 0.01 numbers are the same with or without it, as checked on the smoke run.
    • Markets filters: for the robustness variants, the high-pass runs on the calendar grid for the sky and on the rows for y (window in rows). The primary filter (none) only centres both sides, so it is unaffected. The choice is an implementation detail checked by the calibration. Calibration draws y on random trading-day rows (random weekday rotation, as in Phase 0), with the real calendar offsets on the sky side.
    • Wide calibration grid for markets: 1201-01-01 to 2398-12-31, inside the mundane profile, so that a 98-year window ± 340 years fits.

    Problems¶

    • GDELT, primary filter not calibrated under the measured generator (0.070 at 0.05, KS p 0.017; tolerance 0.066 and 0.02). The rate at 0.01 is within tolerance (0.020 against 0.030). The 180d filter fails too, while 365d and 90d pass. The S2.2 generator is deliberately wider than the real series (synthetic variance +37%, slow AR φ 0.995), so the failure may reflect the generator being more extreme than reality. The rule, though, says a disqualified filter is not used. The plan does not say what happens when the primary filter of a primary arm fails. Switching the GDELT primary to 365d or 90d would change what the confirmatory test estimates, so under Charter § 4.4 it is a post-freeze amendment that I do not make. It is recorded as blocked, with what the PO needs to decide. S3 and the markets runs do not depend on it.
    • GDELT's class 1 (0.0365) and class 2 (0.0469) are above the MEI. This is not a stop condition (the gate is class 0), but the protocol requires declaring it at the opening of the report (PHASE2 § 4).
    • In the result files the protocol stamp reads v0.3: it comes from the engine's transcription of the frozen parameters, which v0.4 did not change for these keys. The step and the v0.4 parameters used are given in the journal.
    • The USGS run went over the session budget. I let it finish rather than lose 58 minutes of computation.
