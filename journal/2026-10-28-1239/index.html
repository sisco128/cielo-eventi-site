    Decisions made (before running what depends on them)¶

    • H3, as announced in the previous entry:
    • the shift moves the exact event instants by whole days, at the same clock time and place;
    • the shift set is the same valid Tier 1 set as H1 (stream spawn(1));
    • there are no weights: each event counts once, as in the oracle's diurnal_resultant;
    • a body–event case is circumpolar when |tan φ · tan δ| > 1, computed with the topocentric declination for the first fast body.
    • Reason: this is the frozen oracle's statistic, with the null of amendment #27 and no extra choice.
    • A set's events are the events whose UTC date falls in the set's months, the same rule as the daily series. Reason: it is the simplest option, and the daily USGS counts use it too.
    • A non-finite observed statistic gets p = 1 (H2 trend, H3 resultant), and non-finite surrogates are left out of the null and counted. Reason: this is the most conservative option. It happens only if a band, or every event, is degenerate.
    • H2 for GDELT and markets only. USGS runs H1 (descriptive) and H3, as amendment #27 lists the arms.
