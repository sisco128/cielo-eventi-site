    Decisions made (written before the tests were run)¶

    • Scope: end to end through runAnalyze, GDELT arm. The unit tests of S3.1 already cover the pieces (RowSky, H2, H3, guard). S3.3 adds tests that call the full runAnalyze with the real sky grid (ephemerides), the real exploration row pattern of the split, and the protocol's shift set (spawn(1), 1000 surrogates). Only y is synthetic. GDELT is used because its grid costs about 2 s; markets (±340-year grid) and the USGS H3 (≈ 13 min per run) are too slow for 50 windows. Markets and USGS are covered by the numeric-contract run below and by their unit tests.
    • Rows. Every calendar day from 2015-02-19 to 2026-09-30 whose month the split assigns to exploration. Only data/split/months.csv is read; no daily series is read.
    • Synthetic y. The measured GDELT generator (results/phase2a/generator.json, drawArm), weekday profile keyed on the rows' real ordinals.
    • Recovery (n = 2, δ = 0.02, ±0.005). The tilt is betaForEffect(c_rows, 0.02) on the aggregate harmonic-2 series at the rows, as in Phase 2a. Two checks: (a) one draw with the generator's noise only (NB around a constant rate, measured mean and k, no structure): |δ̂ − 0.02| ≤ 0.005 and p < 0.05; (b) the full measured structure: the mean δ̂ of 12 independent draws within ±0.005 (a bias check; with the full structure one draw's sd is about the size of the tolerance, per the S2.3 MDE).
    • Calibration under H0 on 50 windows. 50 pseudo-real windows: the exploration row pattern (offsets from 2015-02-19) moved to 50 random calendar starts between 1950-01-01 and 2070-12-31, one independent H0 draw from the full measured generator per window. Criterion, fixed now: for each of the five confirmatory-type tests run on GDELT (H1 aggregate at n = 2, 3, 4, 6 and the H2 trend), at most 7 of 50 p-values ≤ 0.05 (P(X ≥ 8) ≈ 0.003 for Binomial(50, 0.05)); and over the 250 p-values pooled, the share ≤ 0.5 between 0.35 and 0.65 (p not degenerate). Known context: in S2.3 the unfiltered GDELT null gave 0.070 at 0.05 on 400 windows. The test checks the machinery, not that open question; whatever it gives is reported.
    • Determinism. Two runAnalyze calls on the same rows and y give the same JSON, apart from elapsed_s.
    • Numeric contract. pnpm mundane analyze on synthetic files for GDELT (--series) and USGS (--series and --events, 20 invented events): neither stdout nor the written file contains a body, aspect, house or sign name. Added to tests/contract/mundane-numeric-contract.test.ts.
    • S3.4 brought forward in the same session (same phase, inside the budget; Charter § 2). Decisions for holm, written before the code was run:
    • inputs are the three analyze JSONs of one set (gdelt, markets, usgs); holm refuses files from different sets or protocols, or a missing arm;
    • p_H1 = min(1, 8·min p) over the aggregate H1 p of harmonics {2, 3, 4, 6} × {GDELT, markets}; p_H2 = min(1, 2·min p) over the H2 trend p of GDELT and markets; p_H3 = min(1, 8·min p) over the 8 H3 tests; p_H5 = 1. All as amendment #27; Holm step-down at α = 0.05 over m = 5;
    • H4: first Holm runs with p_H4 = 1. If H1 or H3 is rejected, p_H4 is computed and Holm is run again with it (amendment #27: "computed only if H1 or H3 is rejected"). The harmonic is the one of the minimum H1 p over the 8 H1 tests; a tie goes to the lower harmonic (simplest). The statistic is δ̂_GDELT − δ̂_USGS of the aggregate at that harmonic; the null is the same difference under each shift present in both arms' shift lists (same global shift for both arms), upper tail, (1 + #{null ≥ obs}) / (1 + n);
    • to make this possible analyze now also writes, for each H1 aggregate, the shift list and the null δ̂ of each shift (shifts, null_values). This adds output only; no estimate changes.
    • The S2.3 gate for an uncalibrated markets arm is not implemented: markets passed its calibration in S2.3 (0.045 at 0.05, KS p 0.22, results/phase2a/summary.json), so the gate does not apply.
