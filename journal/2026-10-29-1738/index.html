Choices, conservative and with no new estimator: - Null: tier2Shifts() as already in surrogates.ts, i.e. all 78 candidates ±7..±45 days (fewer than N_SURROGATES = 1000, so no draw), global shift as in Tier 1, same for both arms. The Sun with the two slow bodies stays out, as the rule says. - Tests per arm, H1 estimator (same as H1 and E2) at the primary harmonics 2, 3, 4, 6, one-sided upper p: (a) the aggregate over all 18 pairs; (b) the aggregate over the 4 Sun pairs alone. 8 tests per arm, 16 in all. No per-pair Sun tests and no per-class split: fewer tests, and (b) isolates what Tier 2 adds. - Not on H2/H3: the Sun pairs concern the pair harmonics; H3 already has its own null. Reason: the plan names the null (Tier 2) and the extra pairs (the Sun ones); everything else is reused unchanged. Tier 2 carries seasonal drift (protocol § 4.1); the result is secondary and exploratory. - No q-value today. BH-FDR once, after every E1–E7 test of all arms is in. - Implementation detail (does not change any estimate): the E4 pair-set labels in the output are all_admissible and added_only, with no body name (V3).
