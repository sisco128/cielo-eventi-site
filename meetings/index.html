    Calendar¶

    Times in Europe/Berlin; cron in UTC. The research loop and the Friday report keep their own schedules.

    When Meeting Roles Time box Output
    Every day 07:07 (cron 7 6 * * *) Stand-up: sessions since the last meeting, blocks, numbers landed, actions due, infrastructure health Chair, Researcher, Engineer (+ Critic if a new result landed) 20 min meetings/<date>-standup/
    Monday, same run Research meeting: week review, protocol compliance, critic findings, decisions, the week's plan all 45 min meetings/<date>-weekly/
    Tuesday, same run Audit: one module or analysis against the protocol text, from the list in reviews/README.md Critic, Integrity 40 min reviews/<date>-<topic>.md
    Wednesday, same run Infrastructure and scouting: health, what changed in the tooling, RFCs Engineer, Chair 30 min rfcs/
    Thursday, same run Horizon: one increment of the successor study; backlog Horizon, Critic 40 min ../next/
    Friday, same run (the report routine runs separately at 09:00) Open hour: site content, inbox triage under inbox/TRIAGE-RULES.md, public answers drafted Editor, Chair, Integrity 30 min inbox/<week>.md, site/content/
    Saturday, Sunday Stand-up only; under eco, skipped if nothing happened since the last one Chair 10 min
    On demand (API trigger) Huddle: a new blocked; up to three paths, tested against the charter; a class A path or a class B item Engineer, Integrity, Chair 30 min meetings/<date>-huddle/
    First Monday of the month, same run Retrospective: sessions, minutes, blocked hours, PO wait time, usage readings; what to change Chair, Secretary, Engineer 40 min retros/<month>.md
    Every night 23:07 (cron 7 22 * * *), in S6 Night session: one increment of deep work from NIGHT-QUEUE.md (post-S5 audit, independent re-implementation, Horizon, site), alone, in the role the item names one role per night 60 min (eco: 30, Mon–Thu) nights/<date>.md, the item's output
    First Tuesdays and nights after 2026-11-01 Post-S5 audit: S5.1 and S5.2 against the plan and the frozen protocol; holm and H4 against amendment #27; outcome as planned or deviation found (then class B) Critic (max effort), Integrity 2–3 sessions reviews/post-S5.md
    Once, before S7.1 (April 2027) Gate audit: analyze, holm, split and set guard, surrogates, against v0.4 and amendments #16–#29; GO / NO-GO for the PO Critic (max effort), Integrity, Chair 2 sessions reviews/gate-S7.md
    Monday 2026-11-09, in the weekly meeting Addendum check (D7): did the Agent tool work in the cloud, were the time boxes held, did Round 2 take place every day? If yes, council/CHARTER-ADDENDUM.md goes into the Friday report for the PO to merge Chair, Integrity 10 min FOR-THE-PO.md (D7)

    The daily routine (program/routines/council-daily.prompt.md) runs the stand-up every day and then the day's themed meeting in the same session, within the combined time box. Under eco the themed meeting runs only on Monday and Friday. The night routine (council-night.prompt.md) works one queued item and reports to the next morning's stand-up; it pauses in S7–S8, when the research loop is dense again.
